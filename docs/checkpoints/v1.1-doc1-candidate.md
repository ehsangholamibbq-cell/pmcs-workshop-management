## Candidate scope

- independent `documents` schema and explicit module descriptor;
- controlled owner contract for project chat, reports, technical documents, profiles and login assets;
- stable upload sessions, private object storage and server-assigned versions;
- SHA-256, media signature, extension and Open XML package verification;
- replaceable malware-scanner adapter, quarantine, explicit critical release and permission-aware download;
- classification, retention, legal hold and optimistic revision;
- transactional Audit, Outbox and Idempotency receipts;
- classification-safe `documents.asset.released.v1` event;
- actor-scoped IndexedDB upload queue with stable retry identity;
- connected PostgreSQL/MinIO verification for allow/deny, duplicate, version, quarantine, infected content,
  release, download, database state, audit, outbox and idempotency.

## Preserved boundaries

- V1 Evidence semantics and tables are unchanged.
- No general module reads another module's persistence.
- No binary or storage key enters an integration event.
- Chat remains project-group only; private messages, voice and video are outside scope.
- The managerial Agent seven-stage roadmap and advisory/control boundary are unchanged.

## Gate statement

This candidate does not close DOC1 and does not make V1.1 Feature Complete, Qualified or Locked.
Closure requires a successful full CI run, 39-migration restore evidence and a separate evidence
commit. The next stage after DOC1 is `V1.1-IAM1`.
